Tanzania-Focused Driving Questions & Answers

Driver’s License & Registration

  1. How do I apply for a Tanzanian driver’s license?
    Answer: You can apply at the Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA) or Tanzania Licensing Authority (TLA) by submitting your ID, passport photos, and paying the required fees. KAITU Driving School also helps learners prepare for the test.

  2. What are the requirements for a learner’s permit in Tanzania?
    Answer: Applicants must be at least 18 years old, provide valid ID, and pass a medical fitness test.

  3. How long is a Tanzanian driver’s license valid?
    Answer: A standard Tanzanian driver’s license is valid for 3 to 10 years, depending on the category.

  4. Can foreigners get a Tanzanian driver’s license?
    Answer: Yes, foreign nationals can apply using their passport and residence permit. They may also convert their home country license if eligible.

  5. What is the cost of a driver’s license in Tanzania?
    Answer: Costs vary by license type and category. 

Traffic Rules & Safety

  1. What are the main road traffic rules in Tanzania?
    Answer: Key rules include obeying speed limits, wearing seat belts, not using mobile phones while driving, and following traffic signals and road signs.

  2. What is the speed limit in Tanzania?
    Answer: Speed limits are 50 km/h in urban areas, 80 km/h on open roads, and 100 km/h on highways, unless otherwise posted.

  3. Is it mandatory to wear a seatbelt in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, Tanzanian law requires all drivers and passengers to wear seat belts.

  4. What should I do if I am in a car accident in Tanzania?
    Answer: Stop immediately, check for injuries, report the accident to the police, and exchange insurance information with other parties.

  5. Can I drive in Tanzania with an international driving license?
    Answer: Yes, visitors with an international driving permit (IDP) can drive for a limited period, usually up to 90 days.

Vehicle & Road Regulations

  1. How do I register a vehicle in Tanzania?
    Answer: Vehicle registration is done at TRA or TLA offices, requiring proof of ownership, roadworthiness certificate, and payment of registration fees.

  2. Are roadworthy certificates required in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, all vehicles must pass a roadworthy inspection to ensure they are safe for public roads.

  3. What are the rules for vehicle insurance in Tanzania?
    Answer: All vehicles must have mandatory third-party insurance before being allowed on the road.

  4. Can I import a car into Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, imported vehicles must meet Tanzania Revenue Authority (TRA) import rules, pay duties, and obtain a roadworthiness certificate.

  5. What documents must I carry when driving in Tanzania?
    Answer: Always carry your driver’s license, vehicle registration, insurance papers, and identification.

Driving Schools & Learning

  1. Where can I learn to drive in Tanzania?
    Answer: Accredited driving schools like KAITU Driving School provide both theoretical and practical lessons, including preparation for learner’s permits and driver’s license exams.

  2. How long does it take to get a Tanzanian driver’s license?
    Answer: From registration to receiving the license, the process typically takes 2–6 weeks, depending on exam schedules.

  3. Are driving lessons in Tanzania offered in Swahili?
    Answer: Yes, most Tanzanian driving schools, including KAITU, offer lessons in Swahili and English.

  4. Can I take driving lessons for motorcycles in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, motorcycle training is available and required for obtaining a motorcycle category license.

  5. Do Tanzanian driving schools teach defensive driving?
    Answer: Yes, defensive driving is part of the curriculum to improve road safety awareness.

Traffic Fines & Violations

  1. What happens if I drive without a license in Tanzania?
    Answer: Driving without a license is illegal and can result in fines, vehicle impoundment, or legal action.

  2. What are the penalties for drunk driving in Tanzania?
    Answer: Drunk driving can lead to heavy fines, license suspension, or imprisonment.

  3. How much is the fine for speeding in Tanzania?
    Answer: Speeding fines vary by severity but can range from TSh 50,000 to TSh 500,000, depending on the violation.

  4. Are mobile phone use penalties enforced in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, using a mobile phone while driving without a hands-free device can lead to fines and license points.

  5. What should I do if I receive a traffic ticket in Tanzania?
    Answer: Pay the fine at the designated TRA/TLA office or appeal if you believe it was issued unfairly.

Road Safety & Best Practices

  1. What are the common causes of road accidents in Tanzania?
    Answer: Major causes include speeding, reckless driving, drunk driving, poor road conditions, and not following traffic rules.

  2. Is it safe to drive at night in Tanzania?
    Answer: Night driving is allowed, but caution is required due to poor lighting, pedestrian traffic, and animal crossings in rural areas.

  3. Are seat belts required for children in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, children must use approved child restraints or seat belts in vehicles.

  4. Do Tanzanian roads have traffic cameras?
    Answer: Traffic cameras are used in some major cities like Dar es Salaam to enforce speed limits and traffic rules.

  5. How can I drive safely in Tanzania during rainy seasons?
    Answer: Reduce speed, increase following distance, use headlights, and avoid flooded roads.

Public & Commercial Driving

  1. Can I drive a taxi or commercial vehicle in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, you must obtain a commercial driver’s license and comply with public transport regulations.

  2. Are there special licenses for trucks and buses in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, Tanzania issues separate licenses for heavy vehicles and passenger transport.

  3. Do I need a special license for driving a trailer in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, trailers and heavy goods vehicles require additional endorsements on your license.

  4. Are boda-boda (motorcycle taxis) drivers licensed in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, boda-boda drivers must obtain a motorcycle license and register with local authorities.

  5. Can foreigners drive commercial vehicles in Tanzania?
    Answer: Only with a valid local commercial license or a recognized international driving permit.

Miscellaneous Tanzanian Driving Questions

  1. What should I do if my car breaks down on a Tanzanian highway?
    Answer: Turn on hazard lights, move the vehicle safely off the road, and contact local authorities or roadside assistance.

  2. Are there toll roads in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, some highways have toll stations, and fees must be paid before use.

  3. Do Tanzanian traffic rules allow overtaking on hills?
    Answer: No, overtaking is prohibited on hills, bends, and places with poor visibility.

  4. Are helmets required for motorcycle riders in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, wearing a helmet is mandatory for both riders and passengers.

  5. Can I use a GPS while driving in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, GPS is allowed, but it should not distract the driver or block visibility.

  6. Is roadside assistance available in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, many insurance companies and driving schools provide roadside assistance.

  7. Do I need an emissions test for my car in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, periodic emissions and roadworthiness checks are required for vehicle registration.

  8. What is the role of police during traffic checks in Tanzania?
    Answer: Police enforce traffic laws, check documents, and ensure road safety compliance.

  9. Are seatbelt laws strictly enforced in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, fines can be issued for drivers or passengers not wearing seatbelts.

  10. Can I appeal a traffic fine in Tanzania?
    Answer: Yes, fines can be appealed through the traffic court system if you believe the ticket was unfair.
