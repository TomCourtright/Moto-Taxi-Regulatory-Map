Malawi E-Mobility Brief

  • April 2024

Malawi is a bustling, rapidly growing country in Southern Africa with a dense population, cheap electricity, a clean grid, and fiscal incentives for EVs – key ingredients for the e-mobility transition.

In this brief, we unpack the recent policy changes, and highlight the opportunity, gaps, and key areas for future improvements.
